1- All withdrawals from saving accounts may be made by the account owner at the bank's 
   counters. No chequebook will be issued to a saving account holder.
   
2- Interest will be credited on the last balance of account each month. Such interest rate 
   will be determined by the bank and may be changed in accordance with the prevailing rates,
   provided that the monthly minimum balance does not fall below SAR1000. Interest will be 
   credited to the account at the end of June and December each year.
