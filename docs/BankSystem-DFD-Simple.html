A simple view of the read-only enquiry app: who uses it, what it does, and where its data comes from.

Level 0

Context diagram

The app only reads data. It never writes to Denodo or the statement archive.

Level 1

Main processes

The operator logs in once and gets a token. Every request after that carries the token, which 2.0 checks before the enquiry runs.
